## Learning objectives

* Solve linear equations in one unknown using the balance method.
* Solve equations where the unknown appears on both sides.
* Solve equations involving fractions.

## Introduction

```{prf:example}
:label: ex-intro-1
A puzzle — I'm thinking of a number; 8 more than my number is 12. What number am I thinking of?

Let $x$ stand for the number. Then:

$$
x + 8 = 12
$$
```

```{note}
This is an **equation**. The equals sign `=` shows two expressions are equal.

Since the unknown $x$ appears to the first power, this is a **linear equation**.

To maintain equality, we must do the same operation to both sides.
```
